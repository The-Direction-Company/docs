Your dashboard is the home base for your learning journey on The Direction Company. Every time you log in, you land here — where you can pick up where you left off, check your progress, and discover what to learn next.

## Dashboard sections

Each section of your dashboard is designed to surface the most relevant information at a glance, so you spend less time navigating and more time learning.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Continue Learning** | Your in-progress courses, starting with the one you accessed most recently. |
| **Recommended For You** | Personalized course suggestions based on your stated goals and the principles you have been studying. |
| **Progress Overview** | A summary of lessons completed, courses finished, and your current learning streak. |
| **Community Activity** | Recent posts and replies from members in your network and the channels you follow. |
| **Upcoming** | Scheduled live sessions and newly released episodes from courses you are enrolled in. |

## Navigating from the dashboard

Use the sidebar on the left to move between the main sections of the platform — Courses, Community, My Progress, and Account. The top bar stays visible on every page and gives you quick access to notifications and your profile menu. Use the **search bar** at the top of the screen to find courses by principle name, topic keyword, or featured performer — results appear instantly as you type.

## Personalizing your dashboard

You can reorder your dashboard widgets by dragging them into the order that works best for your routine. To pin a course to the top of your Continue Learning section, click the **bookmark icon** on any course card — pinned courses always appear first so your most important work is never buried.

<Tip>
  Check in daily — even 10 minutes of a podclass lesson builds momentum. Your streak counter on the dashboard celebrates consistent learning and resets each day you skip, so make it a habit to open at least one lesson before the day is done.
</Tip>
