The Direction Company community brings together learners who are applying Napoleon Hill's success principles in their own lives. It is a space to share insights, celebrate wins, ask questions, and hold each other accountable — because growth compounds when you pursue it alongside others who are doing the same work.

## Accessing the community

Click **Community** in the sidebar from any page on the platform. The community is organized into dedicated channels so you can find the conversations most relevant to where you are in your journey:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="General Discussion" icon="comments">
    Open conversations about the principles, the platform, and anything related to your growth journey.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Course-Specific Channels" icon="book-open">
    One channel per active course, visible only to enrolled learners. Ask questions, share reflections, and connect with fellow students.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wins & Breakthroughs" icon="trophy">
    Celebrate your progress and cheer on others. Big wins and small ones both belong here.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accountability Partners" icon="handshake">
    Find a partner, set weekly goals, and check in on each other's progress.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Participating in discussions

To start a new conversation, navigate to the relevant channel and click **New Post**. Write your post, add any relevant tags, and submit. Within any thread you can:

- **Reply** directly to a post to keep responses organized
- **React** with an emoji to acknowledge a post without a full reply
- **@ mention** another member to bring them into the conversation

Keep your contributions respectful and relevant to the channel topic. Every channel footer includes a link to the full **Community Guidelines** — review them before posting if you are new to the space.

## Accountability partnerships

The **Accountability Partners** channel is one of the most powerful features of the community. To get started:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Post your goal">
    Create a new post in the Accountability Partners channel describing your learning goal for the week — be specific about which principle you are working on and what action you will take.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get matched">
    Partners are suggested based on shared principle interests and goals. You can accept a suggested partner or reach out to someone whose post resonates with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check in weekly">
    Return to your shared thread each week to report progress. Your partner does the same. Acknowledge each other's updates and adjust goals for the following week.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Course discussions

Every course you enroll in has a dedicated discussion channel that becomes visible to you upon enrollment. Use it to ask questions about a specific lesson, share your reflection with others in the course, or respond to a question that you can help answer. Because these channels are only accessible to enrolled learners, the conversations stay tightly focused on the material.

<Note>
  Community posts are visible to all members of the platform. Do not share personal contact information — including phone numbers, personal email addresses, or social media handles — in public threads.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The most powerful use of the community is the accountability feature. Research consistently shows that shared goals are more likely to be achieved — stating your intention publicly and checking in with a partner creates a layer of commitment that solo learning cannot replicate.
</Tip>
