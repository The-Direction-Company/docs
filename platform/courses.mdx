The Direction Company course library gives you access to every podclass course available on the platform. You can browse by principle, featured performer, or topic — and manage all your enrollments from the Courses section of your account.

## Browsing the library

From your dashboard, click **Courses** in the sidebar to open the full library. Use the filter panel on the left to narrow results by:

- **Principle** — Select from any of Napoleon Hill's 16 success principles.
- **Topic** — Browse by theme such as leadership, wealth, mindset, or resilience.
- **Duration** — Choose Short (under 30 min), Medium (30–90 min), or Deep-Dive (90 min+).
- **Level** — Filter by Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

Each course card displays the principle it covers, the estimated total time, and the name of the featured peak performer so you can decide at a glance whether a course fits what you are working on right now.

## Course detail page

Click any course card to open its detail page. Here you will find everything you need to decide whether to enroll:

When you are ready, click **Enroll Now** at the top of the course detail page to add the course to your library and begin your first lesson.

## Free vs. premium courses

Some courses are available at no cost, while others require an active subscription or a one-time purchase. Free courses display a **Free** badge on their course card and detail page. If you try to enroll in a premium course without the required subscription, you will be prompted to review your plan options. Visit [/account/billing](/account/billing) for a full breakdown of subscription tiers and what each one includes.

## Managing enrolled courses

Open **My Courses** from the sidebar to see all the courses you have enrolled in, sorted by status: In Progress, Not Started, and Completed. On any course card, click the **three-dot menu** to:

- **Unenroll** from the course
- **Add to a list** to organize courses into custom collections
- **Share** the course page with someone else

<Note>
  Completed courses remain in your library forever. You can revisit any lesson at any time — your progress and reflections are saved exactly as you left them.
</Note>
