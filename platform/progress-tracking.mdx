The Direction Company automatically tracks every lesson you complete, every reflection you submit, and every course you finish — so you always know how far you have come and what momentum you are building.

## Lesson completion

A lesson is marked **Complete** when you do two things: finish the audio or video in full, and submit the reflection prompt at the end. If you finish the audio or video but skip the reflection, the lesson is marked **Watched** rather than Complete. Completing both steps is important because the reflection is where the principle moves from something you heard to something you have processed and owned. Completed lessons show a **green checkmark** next to their title in the course outline.

## Course progress

Each course displays a progress bar that updates in real time based on the percentage of lessons you have marked Complete. You can see this progress bar in two places:

- On the **course detail page**, just below the course title
- In **My Courses**, on every enrolled course card

Progress is calculated using Complete lessons only — Watched lessons do not count toward the progress bar percentage.

## Learning streaks

Your streak counter increments each calendar day that you mark at least one lesson Complete. Your current streak is displayed prominently on your dashboard. If you miss a day, your active streak resets to zero — but your full completion history is never deleted or altered. You can always review every lesson you have ever completed in My Progress, regardless of streak status.

## Progress reports

For a full picture of your learning history, go to **Profile > My Progress**. Your progress report includes:

- Total lessons completed
- Total courses finished
- Principles you have studied and how deeply
- Number of reflection entries submitted
- A timeline view of your activity over any date range

You can export your full progress report as a **PDF** at any time — useful for personal reviews or sharing your development with a mentor or coach.

## Milestones and badges

The Direction Company recognizes meaningful achievements with badges that appear on your profile. Earn badges by hitting the following milestones:

<Steps>
  <Step title="First Course Complete">
    Finish all lessons and reflections in your first enrolled course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Principle Track Complete">
    Finish every course that covers one of Napoleon Hill's 16 principles — demonstrating deep mastery of a single principle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Streak Milestones">
    Reach a 7-day, 30-day, or 100-day consecutive learning streak to earn tiered streak badges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your reflection entries are a journal of your growth. Revisit them from **My Progress** to see how your thinking has evolved — the shift in perspective from your first course to your tenth is often striking.
</Tip>
