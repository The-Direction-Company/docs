PM Playbooks are a planned way to package a repeatable product-management routine with its required context, cadence, audience, permissions, and success measures.

## Current availability

No public Playbook catalog or installation workflow is available. This page reserves the documentation location without claiming a shipped capability.

When a Playbook becomes available, its guide must explain what it configures, what connections it needs, who can approve it, how to pause or remove it, and how success is measured.
