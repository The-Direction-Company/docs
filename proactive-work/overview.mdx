Luke is intended to speak first only when a material trigger justifies the interruption. Silence is correct when nothing meaningful changed.

## A proactive message should explain

- why Luke is speaking now;
- what changed or became due;
- the evidence or commitment behind the message;
- the smallest useful action for the recipient;
- how to pause, snooze, dismiss, stop, resume, or change the audience when those controls are available.

## Current availability

Proactive behavior is not generally available. Provider delivery and human evaluation of usefulness and interruption burden remain externally gated.
