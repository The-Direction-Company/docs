<Warning>
  This guide applies only after The Direction Company has confirmed that Luke is enabled for your organization and channel.
</Warning>

## Message Luke

Use ordinary language. Exact command syntax is not required.

> Help me understand the most important product decision we need to make this week. Use the context you can access, show me what is uncertain, and ask only for information you cannot reliably find.

## What happens next

A properly enabled Luke should:

1. resolve the organization, person, conversation, and current authority;
2. retrieve only authorized context;
3. distinguish evidence from assumptions;
4. explain conflicts or missing information that materially affect the decision;
5. recommend a next step while preserving human decision authority.

## Confirm the result

Check that Luke identifies the evidence and limitations behind the recommendation. Correct Luke when context is inaccurate or incomplete.

## Current limitations

Provider delivery, cross-channel continuation, and the complete human pilot remain externally gated. This page does not claim that a specific channel is publicly available.
