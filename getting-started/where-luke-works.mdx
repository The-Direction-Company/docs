Luke's routine interface is the communication channel where the team already works. Connected product, engineering, research, analytics, and customer systems remain authoritative for the records they own.

## One Luke across approved channels

The intended experience is one Luke with consistent product memory, authority, decision history, and follow-through across approved communication surfaces. A provider does not become available merely because an adapter or integration exists.

The Direction Company enables a provider only after verifying:

- organization and person identity;
- membership and permission checks;
- message delivery and recovery;
- revocation behavior;
- conversation continuation;
- audit evidence.

## Current public state

No communication channel is listed as generally available in this initial documentation release. Controlled provider verification and human evaluation remain required before public availability is claimed.
