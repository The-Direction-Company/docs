## Availability labels

- **Current**: approved and available to the stated audience.
- **Preview**: available to a bounded audience with explicit limitations.
- **Externally gated**: provider or human evidence is still required.
- **Planned**: direction only; not available.
- **Unavailable**: not offered in the current product.
- **Deprecated**: present temporarily but scheduled for removal.
- **Removed**: no longer available.
