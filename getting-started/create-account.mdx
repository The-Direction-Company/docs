Creating your account is the first step to gaining clarity and direction. This page walks you through signing up, verifying your email address, and completing your learner profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Visit [thedirectioncompany.com](https://thedirectioncompany.com) and click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner. You can also sign up with Google for a faster start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in your first name, last name, email address, and a strong password (at least 8 characters). Accept the Terms of Service to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from The Direction Company. Click the **Confirm Email** button inside it to activate your account. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Add a profile photo and a short bio — these are optional but recommended for community participation. Select your primary goals from the list provided to help personalize your course recommendations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you signed up with Google, email verification is handled automatically — you can skip straight to completing your profile.
</Note>

## Sign-in options

Once your account is active, you can sign in with your email and password or through Google at any time. If you ever forget your password, click **Forgot Password** on the login page and follow the reset link sent to your email.

## Account security

Keeping your account secure protects your progress, notes, and personal information. Follow these best practices:

- Use a unique password that you don't use on other sites.
- Enable two-factor authentication from your **Account Settings** for an extra layer of protection.
- Always log out of shared or public devices when you're done.
