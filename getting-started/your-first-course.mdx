Your first course is where the journey begins. The Direction Company's podclass format pairs Napoleon Hill's principles with interviews from people who have lived them — giving you practical insight, not just theory.

## Browsing the course library

The course library lists every available course on the platform. Each course card displays the Napoleon Hill principle it covers, the featured peak performer, the estimated completion time, and the difficulty level — so you can find the right fit before you commit. Use the filters at the top of the library to narrow results by principle, topic, or duration.

## Enrolling in a course

Follow these steps to enroll in a course:

1. Click a course card in the library to open its detail page.
2. Read the course overview to confirm it matches your current goals.
3. Click **Enroll Now** to join the course.

Free courses enroll you immediately. Premium courses will prompt you to complete payment or confirm your active subscription before access is granted.

## Completing a lesson

Each lesson uses the podclass player — an audio/video interview built around one of Hill's principles. The player gives you full control over your listening experience:

- **Play/Pause** to start or stop playback at any time.
- **15-second skip** buttons to jump back or forward quickly.
- **Speed adjustment** to set playback between 0.75x and 2x, whichever suits your pace.
- **Captions** toggle for on-screen text as you listen or watch.

At the end of each lesson, a reflection prompt appears. Answer it to mark the lesson complete and lock in what you've learned.

## Saving your progress

Your progress is saved automatically to your dashboard as you move through each lesson. You can close the player at any time and resume exactly where you left off — from any device you use to sign in.

<Tip>
  Take notes during lessons. Use the **Notes** tab in the lesson player to jot down key insights as they come to you. Your notes are saved with each lesson and accessible from your dashboard whenever you want to review them.
</Tip>
