Luke is designed to work through a continuing product conversation rather than a dashboard or rigid command language.

## Core pattern

1. Ask a product question or describe the decision.
2. Let Luke retrieve the context it is authorized to use.
3. Review the evidence, uncertainty, and recommendation.
4. Challenge or correct the reasoning when needed.
5. Record the human decision and rationale.
6. Define a commitment, success measure, or review condition.
7. Let Luke return when the agreed trigger becomes material.

<Info>
  Luke may recommend and explain. A human decision owner remains responsible for consequential decisions and approvals.
</Info>

## Message Luke

> Compare the two options we are considering. Show the strongest evidence for each, the opportunity cost, and what would change your recommendation.

## Current limitations

The complete provider-backed workflow is not generally available. Availability is confirmed per organization and provider.
