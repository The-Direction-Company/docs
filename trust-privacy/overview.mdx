Luke is an AI system. It must remain clearly disclosed as AI and must not claim human identity, biography, emotion, employment, or experience.

## Authority boundary

Models may reason, recommend, summarize, and explain. Deterministic controls are intended to own identity, membership, visibility, permissions, approval requirements, execution, idempotency, recovery, and audit.

Human decision owners remain accountable for consequential decisions.

## Connected-system ownership

A connected provider remains authoritative for the records and actions it owns. Luke should retain provenance rather than silently presenting a copied record as a new source of truth.

## Current public limitations

- No public provider availability is claimed yet.
- No connected-system write capability is publicly offered.
- Formal retention, export, deletion, subprocessor, compliance, and legal statements are not published until approved.
- Do not send credentials, secrets, or sensitive customer data through documentation feedback.
