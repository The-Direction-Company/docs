The fastest path from zero to your first lesson on The Direction Company is three simple steps: create your account, enroll in a course, and press play. From the moment you sign up, everything is designed to get you learning — and growing — as quickly as possible.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [thedirectioncompany.com](https://thedirectioncompany.com) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your name, email address, and a password, then confirm your email by clicking the link we send you. Your account is ready to use immediately after confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a course">
    Browse the course library from your dashboard. Every course is built around one or more of Napoleon Hill's 16 success principles, so each title tells you exactly what you will be working on. Pick the course that resonates most with where you are right now — there is no wrong starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first lesson">
    Click **Enroll**, then open **Lesson 1**. Each podclass lesson is an interview-format session with a modern peak performer who has applied that principle in the real world. Listen actively, take notes, and complete the reflection prompt at the end of the lesson to lock in what you learned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your progress is saved automatically. You can pause and resume any lesson at any time, from any device — just pick up right where you left off.
</Note>

## What's next?

Once you finish your first lesson, keep the momentum going. Head to your [Dashboard](/platform/dashboard) to track your progress and see how far you have come. Visit the [Community](/platform/community) to connect with other learners who are on the same path. And if you want to go deeper into the thinking behind every course, explore the [Success Principles](/concepts/success-principles) to understand the full framework that powers the platform.

<Tip>
  Have a question or need a hand? Reach out to the team any time at [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) — we are happy to help.
</Tip>
