Connected systems remain authoritative for their own records. Luke's product memory is intended to preserve the evidence, assumptions, conflicts, recommendations, revisions, decisions, commitments, and outcomes needed to continue product work responsibly.

## Correct inaccurate context

When Luke uses outdated or incorrect context, state the correction and identify the source that should govern when known.

> The launch date in that recommendation is outdated. Use the approved release decision from last Friday and show me what changes.

A correction should not silently erase the earlier reasoning. The revised recommendation should remain inspectable.

<Info>
  Luke may recommend and explain. A human decision owner remains responsible for consequential decisions and approvals.
</Info>

## Current limitations

The complete provider-backed memory and cross-channel experience is not generally available.
