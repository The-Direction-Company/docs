A podclass is a hybrid between a podcast and a masterclass. The Direction Company created this format to deliver the raw, unscripted insights of real peak performers while anchoring each conversation in Napoleon Hill's proven success principles. The result is a learning experience that feels like a conversation with a mentor rather than a lecture from a textbook.

## Structure of a podclass

Every podclass follows a consistent structure so you always know where you are in the learning journey and what to do with what you hear.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Principle framing">
    Each episode opens with a brief segment that introduces the Napoleon Hill success principle the conversation will explore. You'll understand the core idea and why it matters before the interview begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Peak performer interview">
    The heart of every podclass is an in-depth interview with a peak performer who shares, in their own words, how they applied this principle in their real life — including the struggles, the turning points, and the outcomes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Key insight highlights">
    Throughout the conversation, key insights are called out and summarized so you can track the most important moments without losing the flow of the story.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reflection prompt">
    At the end of every podclass, you receive a focused reflection prompt that asks you to connect the lesson directly to your own current situation, challenge, or goal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why this format?

Traditional courses teach you what to think. The podclass format shows you how to think — through the lens of people who have navigated real obstacles and achieved real success. Hearing an honest, unscripted story activates a different kind of learning than reading a framework in a workbook. You absorb not just the principle, but the texture of how it actually works in a human life. That makes the lesson far more memorable and far more actionable when you face your own challenges.

## Following along

You have several options for how and where you engage with a podclass:

- **Stream in the browser** — Access any podclass directly from your course dashboard without downloading anything.
- **Mobile experience** — The Direction Company is fully optimized for mobile, so you can follow along on your phone or tablet wherever you are.
- **Playback controls** — Adjust playback speed, skip forward or back in 15-second increments, and toggle captions on or off at any time.
- **In-player notes** — Use the built-in notes feature to capture thoughts, quotes, or action items without leaving the player. Your notes are saved to your account and accessible anytime from your dashboard.

<Tip>
  Treat each podclass like a conversation with a mentor. Pause when something lands, rewind to hear it again, and take a moment to reflect before moving on — don't just let it play in the background.
</Tip>
