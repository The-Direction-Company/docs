The Direction Company sends notifications to keep you engaged and informed. You can control exactly what you receive — from daily learning reminders to community reply alerts — from your Notification Settings.

## Accessing notification settings

Go to **Account > Notifications**. Notifications are divided into two categories: **Email Notifications** and **In-App Notifications**. Toggle any option on or off and click **Save Preferences** to apply your changes.

## Email notifications

The following email notification types are available:

| Notification Type | Description | Can Be Disabled? |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning Reminders** | Daily or weekly nudges to continue a course. Frequency is customizable. | Yes |
| **New Course Announcements** | Sent when a new podclass course is published on the platform. | Yes |
| **Streak Alerts** | A reminder when you are at risk of losing your current learning streak. | Yes |
| **Community Replies** | Sent when someone replies to your post or mentions you in the community. | Yes |
| **Billing & Account** | Receipts, renewal reminders, and security alerts. | No |

## In-app notifications

The bell icon in the top navigation bar is your hub for in-app notifications. The following activity types generate in-app notifications:

- **Lesson completions** — confirmation when you finish a lesson or module.
- **Badge awards** — alerts when you earn a new achievement badge.
- **Community activity** — replies, mentions, and reactions to your posts.
- **Accountability partner check-ins** — messages and milestone updates from your partners.

Click the bell icon to see all recent notifications. Click **Mark All as Read** to clear the unread count.

## Unsubscribing from emails

Every marketing and learning email from The Direction Company includes an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom. Clicking it takes you to a preferences page where you can opt out of specific notification types without disabling all emails at once. Transactional emails — including receipts and security alerts — cannot be opted out of, as they are required to maintain your account.

<Tip>
  Set a learning reminder for the same time every day. Consistent scheduling is one of the most effective ways to build a learning habit.
</Tip>
