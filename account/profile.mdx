Your profile is how The Direction Company community sees you. Keeping it up to date helps you get better course recommendations and makes it easier for accountability partners to connect with you.

## Editing your profile

Go to the top-right avatar menu and click **Profile**, or navigate to **Account > Profile**. From there you can update the following:

- **Display name** — the name shown to other learners and accountability partners.
- **Bio** — a short description of yourself (up to 160 characters).
- **Profile photo** — upload a JPEG or PNG image with a minimum size of 200×200px.
- **Primary success goals** — choose the areas you want your course recommendations to focus on.

When you are finished, click **Save Changes**.

## Changing your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Navigate to **Account > Security** and click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password in the **Current Password** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your new password">
    Enter your new password in the **New Password** and **Confirm New Password** fields. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include at least one number and one uppercase letter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Password** to apply the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Linked accounts

Under **Account > Security**, you can link or unlink your Google account at any time. If you originally signed up with Google and want to also enable email/password login, click **Add Password** to set a password without unlinking your Google account.

## Two-factor authentication

Under **Account > Security**, toggle **Enable 2FA** to turn on two-factor authentication. You can secure your account using either method:

- **Authenticator app** (recommended) — apps such as Google Authenticator or Authy generate time-based codes.
- **SMS** — a verification code is sent to your phone number each time you sign in.

Follow the on-screen setup steps to scan the QR code with your authenticator app and save your backup codes in a safe place. You will need a backup code if you ever lose access to your authentication device.

## Deleting your account

Under **Account > Settings**, scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section and click **Delete Account**. You will be prompted to type `DELETE` to confirm. Once confirmed, your account is permanently removed along with all course progress, community posts, and personal data.

<Warning>
  Account deletion cannot be undone. Download your progress report from **Profile > My Progress** before proceeding.
</Warning>
