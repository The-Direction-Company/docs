The Direction Company offers both free and premium content. This page explains how subscriptions work, how to manage your payment method, and how to handle cancellations or refunds.

## Subscription plans

The Direction Company has two plan tiers:

- **Free** — access to all free-tier podclass courses and the full community forum.
- **Premium** — full library access including every course, exclusive peak performer interviews, and downloadable reflection worksheets aligned to Napoleon Hill's 16 success principles.

Current pricing is shown at checkout and on the [Pricing](#) page.

## Managing your subscription

Go to **Account > Billing** to manage all aspects of your subscription. From this page you can:

- View your current plan and upcoming renewal date.
- Upgrade from Free to Premium.
- Update your payment method.
- Cancel your subscription.

## Updating your payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Navigate to **Account > Billing** and click **Update Payment Method**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new card details">
    Type your new card number, expiration date, and security code into the payment form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Payment Method**. Your new card will be charged on your next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Canceling your subscription

Under **Account > Billing**, click **Cancel Subscription**. Your Premium access remains active until the end of your current billing period. After that date, your account automatically reverts to the Free plan. Your course progress, badges, and community history are not affected.

## Refunds

The Direction Company offers a **14-day money-back guarantee** for new Premium subscriptions. If you are within the first 14 days of your initial Premium purchase and would like a refund, contact support at [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) with your order details. Refund requests submitted after 14 days are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## Invoices and receipts

All past receipts are listed under **Account > Billing > Billing History**. Click any invoice row to download a PDF copy for your records.

<Note>
  If you cancel and re-subscribe later, your course progress and community history are preserved.
</Note>
