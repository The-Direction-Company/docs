Luke, from The Direction Company, is an AI product manager designed to work inside the communication channels and connected systems a software team already uses.

<Warning>
  Luke is not generally available yet. Access is enabled only after The Direction Company verifies the organization, provider, permissions, delivery, recovery, and audit path.
</Warning>

The documentation is a reference surface. It does not provide a customer account, dashboard, settings interface, billing interface, support portal, integration manager, or second AI assistant.

## What Luke helps with

Luke is designed to help a team:

- understand product context before asking avoidable questions;
- identify conflicts, assumptions, and missing evidence;
- compare alternatives and recommend direction;
- preserve challenge, revision, rationale, and human decision authority;
- coordinate commitments and review conditions;
- return when a material decision, blocker, or outcome needs attention.
