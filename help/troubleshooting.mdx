If something isn't working as expected on The Direction Company, this guide covers the most common issues and how to resolve them quickly. Work through the relevant steps below before contacting support.

## Lesson won't play or is buffering

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your internet connection">
    Make sure you have a stable connection. Try loading another page or video to confirm your network is working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    A simple refresh clears many temporary playback errors. Press **Ctrl + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + R** (Mac), then try playing the lesson again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different browser">
    Chrome and Firefox are recommended. If you're using Safari or Edge, switch browsers and test again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable browser extensions">
    Ad blockers and privacy extensions can interfere with the media player. Temporarily disable them and reload the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Wi-Fi on mobile">
    If you're on a cellular connection, switch to Wi-Fi. Streaming podclass audio and video requires a consistent bandwidth that cellular networks don't always provide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## I can't log in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Double-check your credentials">
    Confirm you're using the correct email address and that your password is entered exactly as set (passwords are case-sensitive).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    Click **Forgot Password** on the login page, enter your email, and follow the link in the reset email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the correct sign-in method">
    If you signed up with Google, use the **Sign in with Google** button — a password login won't work for Google-linked accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear your browser cache and cookies">
    Cached data can cause login failures. Clear your cache, close and reopen your browser, then try logging in again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support if you're still locked out">
    Email [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) with your account email and a description of the error you see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## My progress isn't saving

This usually happens when a lesson is watched but the reflection prompt is not submitted. You need to complete the reflection prompt to mark a lesson as **Complete** and have it recorded in your progress. Also check that you are logged in — guest views do not save progress to any account.

## I didn't receive a verification email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your spam or junk folder">
    Verification emails sometimes land in spam. Search for an email from **thedirectioncompany.com** and mark it as Not Spam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait up to 5 minutes">
    Delivery can occasionally be delayed. Give it a few minutes before requesting another email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend the verification email">
    Go to the login page and click **Resend Verification Email** to trigger a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your signup email">
    Make sure the email address you check matches the one you used to create your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support if the issue persists">
    Reach us at [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) and we'll verify your account manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification emails aren't arriving

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your spam folder">
    Find a Direction Company email, open it, and mark it as **Not Spam** to train your email client to deliver future messages correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your notification settings">
    Go to **Account > Notifications** and verify that the relevant email type is turned on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add our address to your contacts">
    Add [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) to your email contacts. This signals to most email providers that our messages are trusted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Course content looks incorrect or outdated

If you spot content that seems wrong or out of date, contact support with the course name and the specific issue you've noticed. The team reviews and updates course content regularly, and your feedback helps us keep everything accurate.

<Note>
  For issues not covered here, email [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) with a description of the problem, your browser and device, and any error messages you see. The more detail you include, the faster we can help.
</Note>
