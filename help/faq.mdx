Find answers to the most common questions about The Direction Company below. If you don't see what you're looking for, visit the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) or reach out to our support team.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is The Direction Company?">
    The Direction Company is a podclass-style learning platform built around Napoleon Hill's 16 success principles. Each course features interviews with modern peak performers who share how they've applied those principles in their own lives and careers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a podclass?">
    A podclass is a hybrid between a podcast and a masterclass. Each one pairs a success principle with an in-depth interview with a real peak performer, and ends with a reflection prompt to help you apply what you've heard to your own journey.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the content free?">
    Some courses are free. Premium courses require a subscription. You can see which is which in the course library — free courses are clearly labeled so you always know what you're getting before you commit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I access courses on my phone?">
    Yes. The Direction Company is fully accessible on mobile browsers. Lessons play on any device with a modern browser and an internet connection, so you can learn wherever you are.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Go to **Account > Billing** and click **Cancel Subscription**. You keep Premium access until the end of your current billing period — nothing is cut off immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my progress if I cancel?">
    Nothing. Your progress, completions, and reflection entries are always saved to your account, regardless of your subscription status. You won't lose any of your work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are peak performers selected?">
    Performers are chosen because their stories authentically illustrate one or more of Hill's 16 principles. The emphasis is on depth, honesty, and practical insight — not celebrity or social media following.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I download lessons for offline listening?">
    Downloading is available to Premium subscribers on the mobile app. Free-tier accounts can only stream lessons — they are not available for offline playback on the free plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    Click **Forgot Password** on the login page and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder before requesting another.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email us at [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com) or visit [/help/contact](/help/contact) for all available support options.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
