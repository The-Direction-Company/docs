The Direction Company support team is here to help with account issues, billing questions, technical problems, and anything else you need. Reaching us is simple — and we aim to respond to all inquiries within one business day.

## Email support

Send a message to [support@thedirectioncompany.com](mailto:support@thedirectioncompany.com). For the fastest response, include the following in your email:

- **Your account email address**
- **A description of the issue** — what happened and when
- **The course or page where the issue occurred** (if applicable)
- **Your browser and device** (for technical issues, e.g. Chrome on MacOS, Safari on iPhone)
- **Any error messages or screenshots** you can share

The more context you provide, the faster we can diagnose and resolve your issue without needing to ask follow-up questions.

## In-app help

Click the **?** icon in the bottom-right corner of any page on The Direction Company platform. The help widget lets you search the documentation and submit a support ticket without leaving the app — no need to open your email client or switch tabs.

## What to expect

<Tip>
  Response time is typically within 1 business day. Premium subscribers receive priority support and move to the front of the queue. Complex issues — such as billing disputes or account recovery — may take up to 2–3 business days to fully resolve. We'll always keep you updated if your case requires more time.
</Tip>
