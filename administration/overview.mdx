Luke's customer administration is designed to begin and resolve through messages. There is no Luke customer account, dashboard, settings site, billing site, integration manager, support portal, privacy portal, or admin application.

Planned administrative tasks include inspecting access, changing preferences, managing approved identities, requesting disconnection, and beginning export or deletion requests. Each task must verify current identity and authority before changing state.

## Current availability

No public administrative workflow is available. Do not rely on this page to perform an operation. When enabled, Luke will explain the exact action and current limitation in the conversation.
