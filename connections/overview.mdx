A connection gives Luke authorized evidence or a bounded capability. It does not make Luke the source of truth for provider-owned records.

## Connection contract

Before a connection is publicly documented, its guide must explain:

- what the connection provides;
- the access Luke requests;
- what remains authoritative in the provider;
- who can approve the connection;
- how success, revocation, expiry, and failure are confirmed;
- current limitations and side effects.

<Info>
  When a provider-owned authorization page is technically required, Luke should send the exact handoff in the originating conversation and confirm the result afterward.
</Info>

## Current availability

No provider-specific connection guide is published yet. A guide will appear only after the provider's public state and instructions are approved.
